## Platform

web

## Stack

Astro + Bun + Svelte (user-pinned). Static output, deployed to GitHub Pages via GitHub Actions. Custom domain danieltoft.dk (DNS switch is the owner's step).

## Users

- **Network and recruiters**: people who met Daniel, got his name, or are sourcing senior .NET/architecture profiles. They want a quick read on who he is, what he has done, and how to reach him.
- **Other developers**: peers who land here from GitHub or a talk and judge the site as a developer's site. They must be caught too; the nerdy details are for them.

## Product Purpose

A personal one-pager for Daniel Toft that works as a digital business card: who he is, his work history, and where to find him. Success means a visitor leaves knowing he is a software architect and developer in Aarhus with a long .NET career, and clicks through to email, LinkedIn, or GitHub.

## Positioning

Daniel's own career, told truthfully: a continuous line from 2008 apprentice at Bang & Olufsen to Software Architect at ILLUMI, including years where he ran his own company (SystemaWeb) in parallel with employment. The site shows less than the old one on purpose.

## Operating Context

Read on laptops and phones, often after a LinkedIn message or a GitHub profile visit. A single scroll. Language is Danish.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- One page: short about text, skills, experience list, contact links. Projects/portfolio are next (as `git tag`s on the roles), not yet built.
- Shown: name, title, city, email, GitHub, LinkedIn. Not shown: phone number, photo.
- Experience lists jobs plus education (Tech College Aalborg), which runs as its own `uddannelse` branch. The log's root is an `Initial commit` on the owner's birth date.
- Must be fully readable without JavaScript; motion must respect reduced-motion.

## Product Principles

1. Show less, say it precisely. Every element earns its place.
2. Truth over decoration: nerdy devices must visualize real facts (dates, overlaps, durations).
3. Two audiences, one page: a recruiter gets it in seconds; a developer finds the details rewarding.
4. Contact is never more than one glance away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard reachable, content visible without JS, prefers-reduced-motion honored.
